My Bookshelf

A little peek into my reading world.

Tomorrow I become A Woman

By Aiwanose Odafen.

Literary Fiction

A moving story set in Nigeria, following Obianuju (Uju) as she navigates marriage, family expectations and the challenges faced by women in a traditional society. Through friendship, love and difficult experiences, she learns to find her own voice and strength.

A Thousand Splendid Suns

By Khaled Hosseini.

Historical Fiction

A moving story of two Afghan women, Mariam and Laila, whose lives become intertwined through hardship, loss and war. Despite their difficult circumstances, an unexpected friendship grows between them, revealing the strength of love, sacrifice and the human spirit.

The Seven Year Slip

By Ashley Poston.

Contemporary Romance | Magical Realism Novel

Clementine is trying to move forward with her life when she discovers something unusual about her late aunt's apartment: it connects two different points in time, seven years apart. When she meets a charming stranger living in the past, an unexpected love story begins. A beautiful tale of love, grief, healing and the importance of timing.
